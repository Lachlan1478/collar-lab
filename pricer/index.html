STRUCTURING DESK · FUNDED COLLAR PRICER

Collar Pricer

Fetch the market as of a date, build the structure leg by leg, and read off the financing: notional, lending against the floor, tenor and PV.

European Black-Scholes on 90-day realised vol (flat, no smile) · Asian legs via averaging-vol approximation · illustrative, not a trading system

Market data: Yahoo Finance daily bars via the local server (/api/hist) — spot is the close on the last trading day on or before the start date; ADV and SD are computed over the 90 trading days ending that day, SD annualised (√252). Lending is sized against the put legs: amount = Σ strike × options, discounted per leg at the funding rate.
